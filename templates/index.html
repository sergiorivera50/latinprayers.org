Our Mission

Return to Tradition

What earlier generations held as sacred, remains sacred and great for us too. We wholeheartedly advocate for the expansion of the Tridentine rite, the restoration of traditional Catholic monarchies, and the strengthening of the family. Our ask is that you may pray for these intentions using the tools freely provided in this site.

Special devotion

Pray the Holy Rosary

At Fatima in 1917 Our Lady asked that the Rosary be prayed every day, for peace and for the conversion of sinners. To help you accept this call, we offer an easy-to-follow guide on how to pray the Holy Rosary in latin.

  • Mysteria GaudiosaThe Five Joyful Mysteries
  • Mysteria DolorosaThe Five Sorrowful Mysteries
  • Mysteria GloriosaThe Five Glorious Mysteries

No prayer of the household is more strongly urged upon the family. Consider taking 15 minutes from your day.
